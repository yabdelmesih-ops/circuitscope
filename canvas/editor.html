Build your circuit, then choose Explain node and click any wire or terminal. Drag between terminals to wire, or click a start, corners, then an end. Wire mode lets you branch from existing wires.

Purple: selected node
Orange: entering · Green: leaving

    E: Explain node · W: Wire · Tab: flip bend · Backspace: remove corner · Esc: move mode. In Wire mode, click a wire to create a branch dot. Crossings alone stay unconnected. Pin 1 → pin 2 defines positive current; pin 1 is + on a voltage source.
